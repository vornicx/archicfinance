Terms of Use

Scope

The service may retrieve balances and transaction information for the purpose of generating personal financial summaries and analysis.

No payment initiation

Archic Finance does not initiate payments, transfers, withdrawals, trades, or any other movement of funds.

No financial advice guarantee

Generated summaries and recommendations are informational and do not constitute regulated financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. The account holder remains responsible for financial decisions.

Availability

The service depends on third-party banking, Open Banking, hosting, and email services and may be temporarily unavailable or require renewed authorization.

Security

The account holder must keep authorization credentials, application keys, and access tokens confidential. Bank login credentials should never be stored in the application.
