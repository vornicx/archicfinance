Privacy Policy

Archic Finance is a personal, read-only financial monitoring application used by its owner to review account balances and transaction history through regulated Open Banking providers.

Data processed

The application may process account identifiers, balances, transaction amounts, transaction dates, merchant or counterparty information, and related transaction metadata made available through the connected Open Banking provider.

Purpose

Data is processed solely to provide personal financial summaries, spending analysis, cash-flow monitoring, and related financial insights to the account holder.

Access and security

The application uses read-only account information access. It does not initiate payments, transfers, or modify bank accounts. Banking credentials are not stored by Archic Finance.

Data retention

The current implementation does not maintain a separate long-term transaction database. Financial data is retrieved as needed to generate summaries.

Third parties

Open Banking connectivity is provided through Enable Banking and the account provider. Email delivery may be provided through Resend. Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy obligations.

Contact

For privacy-related matters, contact the email address registered for the Archic Finance Open Banking application.
